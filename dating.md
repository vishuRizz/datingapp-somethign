# SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM - DATING APPS ARE BROKEN

## **Why People Are Quitting Dating Apps**

### **Problem #1: Fake Profiles & Scams (CRITICAL)**
- **The Issue:** 90%+ of dating apps have fake profiles
- **The Pain:** Users spend weeks chatting with fake people
- **The Cost:** Romance scams cost victims ₹10+ lakhs each year in India
- **The Data:** "Catfishing" is the #1 reason users quit apps

**User Quote:** *"I don't know who's real anymore. I've been catfished twice in 3 months."*

---

### **Problem #2: Ghosting Epidemic (68% of Users)**
- **The Issue:** People disappear mid-conversation without explanation
- **The Pain:** Creates anxiety, rejection trauma, low confidence
- **The Data:** TrulyMadly 2023 survey: 68% of Indian users have been ghosted
- **The Result:** Users feel disrespected, stop using the app

**User Quote:** *"We texted for a week, had great chemistry, then they just vanished. It messes with your head."*

---

### **Problem #3: Superficial Matching (Swipe Fatigue)**
- **The Issue:** Endless swiping through strangers based on photos
- **The Pain:** "Paradox of choice" - too many options = decision fatigue
- **The Data:** 42% of users feel overwhelmed by the number of matches
- **The Result:** Poor quality matches, wasted time, high churn

**User Quote:** *"I swipe through 1000 people and still can't find anyone I actually click with."*

---

### **Problem #4: Gender Imbalance & Harassment**
- **The Issue:** Far fewer real female profiles than male profiles
- **The Pain:** Men get almost no matches. Women get harassed constantly.
- **The Data:** Match rates show 80/20 disparity (men struggle, women overwhelmed)
- **The Result:** Both genders frustrated, app feels broken

**User Quote:** *"As a guy, I get maybe 3 matches a month. As a girl, I get 100 creepy messages a day."*

---

### **Problem #5: No Real Connection (High Churn)**
- **The Issue:** Apps optimized for engagement, not relationships
- **The Pain:** Users stay on longer, but leave dissatisfied
- **The Data:** 60% monthly churn rate (users quit after bad experiences)
- **The Result:** Retention is terrible for the app, worse for users

**User Quote:** *"All my dates from apps were disappointing. There's no real connection happening."*
